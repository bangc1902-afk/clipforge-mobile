Limitations

  • Does not download YouTube watch-page links.
  • Does not detect highlights by itself; Claude must inspect the video or use a timestamped transcript.
  • Large files may exceed Android browser memory. Start with shorter videos.
  • Does not currently burn captions, add music, or auto-reframe to vertical.
  • Only edit material you own or have permission to use.
